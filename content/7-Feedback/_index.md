> Here, you can freely share your personal opinions about your experience participating in the First Cloud AI Journey program. This will help the FCAJ team improve any shortcomings based on the following aspects:

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment** 
The atmosphere at FCAJ is highly collaborative and welcoming. Team members consistently offer their support, even after standard hours, to help overcome technical blockers. The physical workspace is well-organized, promoting productivity. However, integrating more team-building activities or casual get-togethers would further enhance the team dynamic and inter-departmental connections.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin** 
The mentoring quality is exceptional. Mentors don't just hand out answers; they encourage independent problem-solving and critical thinking while providing clear explanations when I'm truly stuck. Meanwhile, the admin team ensures smooth operations by handling paperwork and logistics efficiently, allowing me to focus entirely on my engineering tasks.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major** 
The assigned projects perfectly bridged the gap between my university curriculum and real-world application. Not only did I get to apply the theoretical foundations I learned in school, but I was also exposed to cutting-edge cloud technologies and practical scenarios I had never explored before.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities** 
Beyond coding, this internship significantly leveled up my soft skills, including Agile project management, cross-functional collaboration, and professional workplace communication. The real-world industry insights shared by my mentors have also given me a clearer perspective on my future career trajectory.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit** 
FCAJ fosters a culture of mutual respect and balanced professionalism. The work environment is focused yet highly enjoyable. During crunch times or tight deadlines, the entire team rallies together, regardless of titles or seniority. This inclusive spirit made me feel like a valued core member rather than just a temporary intern.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits** 
The program offers a solid compensation package for interns, including a financial allowance and the flexibility to adjust working hours when necessary. Furthermore, the exclusive access to internal training workshops was a massive benefit.

### Additional Questions

**What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**
The most fulfilling aspect was the exposure to a wide variety of technical domains (from backend development to cloud infrastructure and AI integration). This cross-domain experience significantly broadened my worldview regarding the software engineering industry as a whole, showing me how different pieces of the puzzle fit together in enterprise environments.

**What do you think the company should improve for future interns?**
The training video tutorials need a complete overhaul. Currently, the videos demonstrate isolated configurations of single AWS services, which feels dry and disconnected from real use cases. Instead, the curriculum should focus on small, project-based tutorials with clear goals and business purposes. This would provide genuine hands-on experience in cloud architecture and infrastructure design, rather than just blindly mimicking configuration steps on the AWS Console.

**If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they intern here? Why or why not?**
I would recommend this program to peers who are struggling to find a solid tech company locally or if other tier-1 opportunities are too geographically distant. Otherwise, whether I recommend it depends heavily on their specific career goals and workstyle preferences. 

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

**Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**
Transitioning the onboarding and training phases entirely towards project-driven learning, rather than isolated service tutorials.

**Would you like to continue this program in the future?**
I am very open to participating in future FCAJ technical workshops, webinars, or remote sync meetings to stay connected with the community and continue learning. However, I am not inclined to continue working in a strict on-site office capacity, as I plan to focus on remote opportunities moving forward.
